PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Effective November 8, 2026. This policy covers the Homelab Power website and the optional Android app.

No account and no server

Homelab Power does not have accounts, analytics, or a backend. The calculator runs in your browser. It does not send your lab to a server operated for this app.

What stays on this device

Devices, electricity rates, schedules, circuit settings, theme, and any saved labs are stored in this browser’s local storage. An optional Pro preview flag is stored the same way. Clearing the site’s data in the browser deletes them. If you install the app, a service worker keeps a copy of the pages on the device so it can open offline. That copy is the app itself, not an upload of your lab.

Share links

Copy share link puts your lab in the URL’s hash: device names, watts, rates, schedules, and circuit settings. Anyone with that link can read those details. The hash is not sent to this site as a separate upload, and it is not included when you follow a link to another website. It does stay in the address bar and in this browser’s history. Only share a link if you are comfortable with what it shows.

CSV files

Download CSV saves a file on your device. The file is not uploaded. If you later import a CSV, that also happens in the browser.

Links to rate sources

Country rates cite public pages from the U.S. Energy Information Administration and Eurostat. Opening one of those links leaves Homelab Power. Those sites have their own policies. The lab in your address bar’s hash is not sent with the click.

Android app, Pro, and tips

The Android version of Homelab Power can offer an optional Pro unlock and a supporter tip through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment. The app does not receive your card number or other card details. Purchases are tied to your Google account and are covered by Google’s privacy policy. The free calculator does not require a purchase.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the project’s GitHub issues page.
