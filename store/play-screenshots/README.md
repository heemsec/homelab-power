The planner shot is Nick's 8U build, from the name and the yearly cost down through the top of the rack. The savings shot is the Dell R730 preset, with a $120 tiny-PC swap. The calculator shot is the sample lab: a rack server, a 4-drive NAS, a mini PC, a switch, and a Pi.
