PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Effective November 8, 2026. This covers the website and the optional Android app.

No account, and no server

There's no sign-in. No analytics either. Homelab Power doesn't keep a backend for your numbers. The calculator is a page in your browser, and it doesn't upload the lab.

What stays on this device

Devices, rates, schedules, circuit settings, the theme, and any saved labs sit in local storage on this device. The Pro preview flag does too, if you turn that on. Clear the site data and they're gone.

Install the app and a service worker keeps a copy of the pages so it can open offline. That's the app itself, not your lab being sent anywhere.

Share links aren't private

Copy share link puts the lab in the URL hash. Names, watts, rates, schedules, and circuit settings are all in there. Anyone with the link can read them.

The hash isn't sent to this site as its own upload. Other sites don't get it when you click away, either. It does stay in the address bar and in this browser's history. Only share a link if you're fine with what's in it.

CSV files stay local

Download CSV writes a file on your device. Nothing is uploaded. If you import a CSV later, that happens in the browser too. A JSON backup file is the same idea, and bringing that file back is part of Pro. The copy already saved in this browser stays either way.

Those rate links leave this page

Country averages point at public pages from the U.S. Energy Information Administration and Eurostat. Their policies are theirs. The lab in your address bar isn't sent along, because a URL hash isn't part of the request.

Android, Pro, and tips

The Android app can offer an optional one-time Pro unlock, and three optional tips, through Google Play Billing. A tip doesn't unlock Pro or anything else, and you can send one more than once. Google handles the payment. The app never sees your card number or the rest of the card details. Purchases sit on your Google account, under Google's privacy policy. You don't need to buy anything to use the calculator, the planner, or Settings.
