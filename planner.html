How long a UPS might last

A published curve is for a new, full battery. The low end of that range is 60% of the curve, because an older pack often falls short. If the sheet only gives battery energy, the middle guess uses 85% inverter efficiency and 80% of the pack. The wide range runs from 80% efficient and 70% of the pack to 90% and 90%. Age, charge, and the room all move the real number.
